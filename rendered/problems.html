5 reports count twice: under their cause by tenant, where the desk files them, and as a one-day move.

The problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so a tenant that writes the day first gets day and month swapped: a 7 April reminder goes out on 4 July. Any channel, any tenant on file. This is the fix #4471 was waiting for.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as told, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant. The parser fix does not reach it: the hint has to match the tenant.
  • one-day move: the date moves by one day, with no month swap. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon got Tuesday and one in Toronto got Monday. Neither the parser nor the hint explains that; it looks like a time zone shift. The desk read these apart (desk-notes.json), but the #4471 table still counts them under parser.

Where they stand

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October.

  • parser: fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket closed.
  • hint: won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired, so no new date comes in through its hint. Ticket closed as not planned.
  • one-day move: open. Neither change moves a date back by one day.

How the reports were split

  • One report per id. The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice in the file.
  • By tenant, not by intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale, its booking form and the channel the report came in (causes.ts).
  • Not by subject. The same subject turns up in more than one problem, and one problem comes in under many subjects.
#4471 is closed, but the one-day move is not fixed. Keep its ticket open until it is.
